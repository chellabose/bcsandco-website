Chelladurai B

Founder and Managing Partner

Qualifications

Member of ICAI since

Areas of practice
Direct and indirect taxation, company law matters

Chelladurai B has been a member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India since 2012 and is a Fellow member. He is also a member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India, has completed the Intermediate course in Cost and Management Accountancy, and is a commerce graduate of Vivekananda College.

He worked in direct taxation at Aircel Limited from 2012 to 2014, then in taxation at the Archean Group, and later as Head of Accounts at Stellar Plastics India Private Limited. He began practice as B C S & Co. in 2016.

He holds certifications in Forensic Accounting and Fraud Detection, Advanced Goods and Services Tax, Concurrent Audit of Banks, and Public Finance and Accounting.
